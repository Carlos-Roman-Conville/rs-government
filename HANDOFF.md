## Gotchas

- Spending takes laws in enactment order; host supplies treasury context and receipts.
  paid means commanded allocation, not ledger confirmation; host prevents period replay.

- Tax bases return {amount, unit}; hosts execute collection commands and supply
  confirmed totals to taxBreaches at the deadline. Missing outcomes are unknown.
- Law files cite `procedure.<slug>`; procedure records are `government/procedure.<slug>`;
  `checkLaws` maps the prefix.
- Succession host supplies office {id, place}, recognition and per-law choices
  ([] = none, null = unknown). No preference default; no direct heir stays unresolved.
- No stored government type or legitimacy meter. Settlement owns offices and money;
  Government emits commands and never writes owner state.
- Supersession is same-authority-only. Null issuer/authority: own unstated:<law id>
  set, never supersedes, J = 0; delegation skips it. A jurisdiction is not a place.
- From is inclusive (null = unbounded); until is exclusive; year-only means Jan 1.
  Null until does not prove perpetual validity.
- Notes never execute; empty sanctions invent no remedy.
